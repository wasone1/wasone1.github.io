EV Charge Pro

A precision charging calculator for electric vehicles and portable power stations. Estimate charging time, energy usage and total cost based on real-world parameters.

Download App

Interface Preview

Features

  • Smart Session Tracking: Automatically log every charge.
  • Cost Calculator: Real-time cost estimation based on tariffs.
  • Battery Health: Analytics to prolong your battery life.
  • Export Data: CSV export for accounting.

Calculation Engine

The algorithm uses manufacturer-based battery capacity ranges, charger power limits, electricity price and charging efficiency.
