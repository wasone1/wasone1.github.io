Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Multi Timer app (hereby referred to as "Application") created by OTB dev. as a Free service.

Information Collection

The Application collects minimal information necessary for functionality:

  • Device Information: We may collect generic device info (model, OS version) for crash reporting and optimization.
  • Local Data: Your timer presets and names are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this personal data to any server.

Permissions

To function correctly, the Application requires specific permissions:

  • Notifications: Required to alert you when a timer finishes, even if the app is in the background.
  • Alarms & Reminders: Exact alarm API is used to ensure precision timing.

Third Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics and stability improvement:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
