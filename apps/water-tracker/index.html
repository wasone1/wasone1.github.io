Water Tracker

An app designed to easily monitor your daily water intake. It helps maintain proper hydration, motivates a healthy lifestyle, and keeps your body energized by replenishing your water balance on time.

Pre-test

Interface Preview

Features

  • Flexible Tracking & Goals: Set custom daily targets and log your hydration effortlessly.
  • Smart Notifications: Customizable reminders so you never forget to drink water.
  • History & Achievements: Track your progress, review history, and earn motivational rewards.
  • Reliability & Convenience: Data backup (save/load functionality) and multilingual interface support.

Health & Motivation

Control over your water intake allows you to independently set goals, maintain body tone, and stay refreshed throughout the day.
